## decision-0003: X's look, with icon-only tabs

- Date and origin: 2026-11-15; agent choice.
- Choice and rationale:
  - The palette follows X's mobile app, with its own colors for like and repost.
  - The tabs show icons only, with labels for screen readers. The chosen tab fills its icon over a pill.
  - The tab bar shows only on tab roots, as `motion.md` asks. X itself keeps it on pushed screens, so this differs from X.
- Evidence and supersession: static only; none superseded.

## decision-0004: Save and Manage as sheets

- Date and origin: 2026-11-15; agent choice.
- Choice and rationale:
  - `save` and `manage` draw as bottom sheets in their own stage. The screen below stays in place and is inert, and the sheets rise in, swap and close as `motion.md` describes.
  - `x-bookmarks` used full screens because of the keyboard. X2 follows the native-feel overlay guidance instead.
  - Affects the Categories journeys.
- Evidence and supersession: static only. The keyboard over a sheet is unverified on a device.

## decision-0005: An in-app Discard dialog

- Date and origin: 2026-11-15; agent choice.
- Choice and rationale: leaving a draft asks "Discard post?" in an in-app dialog through the router's `onConfirmLeave`, instead of the browser's `confirm()`, so the dialog matches the app and keeps its theme.
- Evidence and supersession: static only; none superseded.
