# Moving skeletons and a working Try again

- Goal: The UX pass on 2026-11-15 found still skeletons that read as frozen, and a Saved or Categories error with no way to retry once the bookmarks service came back.
- Changes: Skeleton rows fade in after 120 ms, so a fast load never flashes them, then shimmer with a new `--shimmer` theme token, light and dark. The Saved and Categories error notices get Try again, which calls `storage.refresh` through the library's new `retry`. The button reads "Trying again…" and is disabled until the read answers, for at least 800 ms from the tap so an instant failure still shows the tap worked, and gives up after 4 s. Search results and Home after a search now show their own screen at once through the SDK router's pending pushes, with no applet change.
- Decisions: Routine polish within the defaults' loading and error rules; no new decision.
- Verification: `check` and `build` pass. On the Samsung SM-M307F with the Genesis debug build of `fix/applet-ux-polish`: Search goes straight from the form to the results skeleton (three tries, one from a cold start), Home after a search shows the feed skeleton then the feed, and Saved's Try again shows "Trying again…" with the service stopped. The bookmarks service was not running, so a successful retry was not seen.
- Release: version 0.0.4, published to `published/` with Genesis `f3f32cce9f2f`, not tagged.
