# Search Hacker News

- Goal: Replace the Search placeholder with a working search of HN stories and comments, as the developer's everyday-features plan asked.
- Changes: The manifest adds the `https://hn.algolia.com` origin, a `search` route on the `primary` context and a `results` screen. The new `search` adapter page reads story and comment results, the result count and the next page. The Search tab fills its field with the last search. The results screen has the field, Stories and Comments filters, Popular and Newest sorts, Next page, a "No results" notice that offers to search comments, and the skeleton and error states. Both "1 points" and "1 comments" now read in the singular.
- Decisions: decision-0007. The three-context limit rules out a separate search context, so results use Home's context, and the Search tab stays highlighted while it shows them.
- Verification: `typecheck`, `check`, `build` and `npm test` passed. Tested on a Samsung SM-M307F, signed out, with dev revision 6. Every interaction above worked, and Back from a discussion kept the results' scroll. A slow or offline search site was not tested.
