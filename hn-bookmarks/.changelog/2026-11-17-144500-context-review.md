Problem ID: `applet-performance` · Problem: Find what makes page switches, loads and like/save actions lag in the built applets · Spirit animal: 🐆 Leopard

- Goal: Decide whether the `post` context earns its own page, now that Genesis rests pages the applet is not using.
- Changes: none in code. The decision is recorded.
- Decisions: decision-0016 keeps `post` and `me`.
- Verification: on a phone with this applet built on the current SDK: the feed, the Post and Me tabs, a story with its comments and Back work on the first tap; the pages start about 3.6 s apart; idle on the feed costs 4 to 9 ms per second.
