# App icon and drawer entry

- Goal: Make Hacker News look like its own app now that Genesis runs each applet in its own window with its own Recents card, shortcuts and drawer entry.
- Changes: `assets/icon.png`, a 512 px original bookmark-ribbon icon with a transparent background, and `shell.icon` in `applet.json`. The Genesis repository's bundle selection gives the applet an app drawer entry.
- Decisions: decision-0010.
- Verification: `genesis-applet check` passes, so the icon is a square PNG of an allowed size. Device: checked on 2026-11-13 on the Samsung SM-M307F (Android 11), signed in, with the Genesis debug build of the same branch. The app drawer shows a Hacker News entry with the ribbon on orange; it opens the applet in its own window with the feed. Its Recents card shows the icon and accent. The pinned shortcut from Genesis's "Add to home screen" opens the feed. The Genesis applet grid, Applet info and the splash show the icon on its orange accent, after a Genesis fix: the transparent icon had been drawn alone, white on white. Not checked: notifications, which this applet does not use.
