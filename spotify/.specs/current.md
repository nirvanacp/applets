## Product scope

Goal: a Spotify applet that feels like the native Spotify app while running the Spotify web player (open.spotify.com) underneath. Source of requirements: the user's PRD (58 screens, about 150 requirement IDs in groups ON, HM, SR, PB, QU, DV, JM, MS, BL, LB, AR, PD, AB, PR, ST, NF) and its PNG designs 1.1 to 12.5. Agreed delivery: phase 1 covers what the web player supports (onboarding and sign-in, Home, Search, Now Playing with mini-player, Queue, Devices, Library and playlists, Album and Artist, Podcasts, Audiobooks, Profile, Settings the web exposes); features the web lacks are recorded as blocked. Phase 2 (sleep timer, share sheet, applet-local extras) is proposed later.

Status: the PRD build runs in six milestones, each verified on the phone and committed before the next (decision-0004). Milestone 1, the app frame and player, is done: tab bar, mini-player on every screen, Now Playing with save, seek, previous, play, next, shuffle and repeat, and "Next up". Milestone 2, Album, Playlist and Artist pages with play, save, follow and track play, is done. Milestone 3, Search, Browse all and category hubs, is built and mostly verified (see Verification). Milestones 4 to 6 (Library, track menu and toasts; Podcasts, Audiobooks, Devices and Lyrics; Profile, Settings and the blocked list) follow.

## Contexts

| Context | Start URL |
| --- | --- |
| primary | https://open\.spotify\.com/ |

The app holds `primary` at `running` while this page plays (decision-0011), so tracks change on time while the user browses.

One context browses and plays (decision-0013). A second page that only plays would be a second Spotify Connect device, so the applet does not add one. Navigation keeps the audio only while `primary` stays in one document, so every Spotify URL the applet opens (tabs, cards, bylines, links from Now Playing) goes through `navigate({ url, sameDocument: true })`: Genesis follows a link the page has for the URL, then tries a history push and `popstate`, and loads a new document only when neither moves the page. Back is the website's own history step, so it stays in the document too (gap P-01). A link in Now Playing closes the full player first, waiting up to 1 s for it to go, and then opens the page (gap C12). Pull to refresh and the Refresh button reload the page only when nothing plays here; while Spotify plays in this page they resync the watches instead, because a reload would stop the audio.

## Screens

- Frame (`src/app.tsx`): bottom tabs Home, Search and Your Library with labels, and the mini-player above them on every screen except Now Playing. Dark only, Spotify's palette as theme tokens (`--canvas` `#121212`, `--accent` `#1ed760`, `--player` `#2b2440`; decision-0006). Screens change through the kit's Stage: forward pushes, Back pops, tab switches fade through, and Now Playing rises over the tabs and falls away (decision-0007). Reselecting the current tab on its first screen scrolls it to the top. A toast region sits above the mini-player and tabs; command outcomes go there (decision-0008). Every header keeps the shell's menu corner clear.
- States: each routed screen draws, in order, signed out ("Log in to Spotify", with a Log in button that carries the website cue), error with nothing to show ("Couldn't load …", Try again and View website with the cue), confirmed empty (with Refresh), its own skeleton, then content. Content that is kept while a refresh fails shows "Couldn't refresh. Showing the last copy." A thin progress bar shows after 250 ms while the page reloads or shows a kept copy. Content that replaces a skeleton fades in.
- Refresh: Home, a category hub, an album, playlist or artist page and Browse all have pull to refresh and a labeled Refresh button in the header (decision-0010). While Spotify plays in this page, refresh resyncs the watches instead of reloading the page.
- `home` (2.1): the greeting as the header title (a skeleton line until Spotify draws it, "Home" once the page is ready without one) and one horizontal shelf per Spotify row, in page order. A tile opens its page by URL, or taps the website card when Spotify gives no link; such a tile dims with a spinner until the page moves, and a failure shows a toast.
- Mini-player: artwork, title and artist ("Playing on another device" in green when Spotify plays elsewhere), a save toggle, Play/Pause and a thin progress line along its bottom edge. It draws from the playback store (decision-0017): a tap shows the requested state in the same frame, and the controls are never disabled while Spotify answers. Saving says "Added to Liked Songs"; removing says "Removed from Liked Songs" with Undo. The track opens Now Playing. A swipe along the track skips, as in the official app: left is Next and right is Previous. The text follows the finger; past 56 px or a quarter of the width it skips and the text comes back from the other side, and a shorter swipe springs back. Under reduced motion the text does not move and the skip still happens. The swipe ends without opening Now Playing. Screen reader users skip from Now Playing, whose buttons carry the names. The bar keeps its place and height while pages load, leaves only after 10 s with no track or at once on sign-out, and is hidden only under Now Playing.
- `player` (4.1): rises over the app and draws the current track from the playback store at once, with no skeleton. It asks the store to open the website's full player on mount and releases it on leave; the full player closes 1.5 s later when nothing else waits, and opening Now Playing in that time finds it open (decision-0019). "Nothing is playing" shows only when the page has no track. If the full player does not open, a note says so with Try again. "Playing from" links to the album or playlist; title and artist link to their pages, and a link closes the full player before it opens its page. Until the full player reports, the source line, shuffle and repeat show their last values for the same track; shuffle and repeat open the full player before they press. Save (with the same toasts and Undo), a progress bar that shows the scrubbed time while dragging and seeks on release, time labels, shuffle, previous, play/pause, next, repeat with off, all and one (a green dot under Shuffle and Repeat while either is on, as in the official app), and Share, which opens Android's share sheet with the track's link. The time labels and the bar run on a local clock. Controls are never disabled for busy: each tap shows its result at once and queues behind the one before. A command Spotify has not confirmed after 6 s falls back to what Spotify shows, with one toast for the burst (decision-0017). A drag down from the top of the screen's scroll follows the finger and closes past a quarter of the height or on a flick, and a shorter drag springs back; a swipe on the art skips, left for Next and right for Previous, as on the mini-player. Under reduced motion neither follows the finger and both still act. "Next up" shows the next track's title, the only one the web player exposes.
- `entity` (8.1 Album, 7.5 Playlist, 8.2 Artist): one screen for the three pages, with a header bar laid over the art that turns solid and shows the title once the header scrolls away. Album and playlist show centred art, title, a playlist's description, the byline (album artists or playlist owner, linking to their pages) and a meta line ("Album · 2006 · 7 songs · 33 min 6 sec"); an artist shows a full-width photo with the name over it and the monthly listeners. The action row has Follow (artist) or the save switch (album, playlist), Share for an album, playlist or artist (Android's share sheet with the page's link; Liked Songs has none) and a green Play/Pause for the whole page, each busy until Spotify answers. Saving says "Added to Your Library"; removing says "Removed from Your Library" with Undo; unfollowing says "Unfollowed …" with Undo. Track rows play on tap and light as a whole while pressed, show a spinner on the row while it starts and the playing row in green; artist rows are numbered with play counts. Then an artist's "Popular releases" list and the page's rows of tiles ("More by …", "Albums", "Fans also like"). Reaching the end of a playlist loads Spotify's next rows, with a spinner at the end of the list. A liked song shows a small green tick before its artist. Each row has a ⋮ button, and a long press on the row opens the same menu: a sheet with the track's art, title and artist, then Add to Liked Songs or Remove from Liked Songs, Share (Android's share sheet with the track's link), View artist and View album. A page hides its link to itself. Like closes the sheet and shows the new state at once; the toast says "Added to Liked Songs" or "Removed from Liked Songs" with Undo. View artist and View album show a spinner until the page opens, and a failure closes the sheet with a toast. Back, a tap outside and a drag down close the sheet. Album rows have no art of their own, so the sheet shows the album's.
- `search` (3.1 to 3.3): on `/search`, the "Search" header, a white search field and the Browse all grid of coloured category tiles; a tile opens its hub. The field opens Spotify's own search page (busy until it does), then shows the applet's field with Cancel. An empty field lists recent searches with a remove button each and "Clear recent searches"; both confirm with a toast and have no Undo (decision-0009). Typing sends the query to Spotify once typing pauses for 350 ms, with the progress bar while results are pending; results show Spotify's filter chips (the tapped chip shows its new state at once), a Top result card (with Play for a song) when no filter is on, and rows titled with "Type · details". A song row plays in place; any other row opens its page; a tapped row shows a spinner over its art.
- `hub` (3.4): a category's name as the header title and its rows of tiles, read by the same `sections` reader as Home.
- `library` (on `/collection`): "Your Library" with a Liked Songs row ("Playlist") that opens Liked Songs on the entity screen. The Your Library tab opens `/collection` in the same document, so playback keeps going.
- Liked Songs (`entity` with the kind `liked`): Spotify's heart tile as its art, the title, "Playlist · N songs", Play, and the song rows. The Your Library tab stays selected on it.

## Page module

`src/page.ts`, with the code in `src/adapter/`:

- `account`: signed in when the website shows its settings gear or the mini-player's save button; signed out on its login button or link.
- `ready.home`, `heading` and `sections` (list, keyed by row title with "#n" for repeats; each row is `{ title, cards }` with at most 20 cards, extent its `h2`). Cards come from `home-card`, `card-mwp` and Encore `card` elements.
- `nowPlaying` (value, measured): `{ title, artist, image, playing, saved, elsewhere, next, previous, progress }` from the mini-player. Its carousel holds the previous, current and next tracks; the current one is the slot whose left edge sits nearest the carousel's left edge. `next` and `previous` carry the title only, because the neighbour slots' artist lines can lag behind. `progress` comes from the bar's style variables `--animationDelay`, `--finalAnimationDuration` and `--animationPlayState`, plus the animation's start time while it runs, which gives the moment the track was at 0 (decision-0016).
- `player` (value): the open full player's track, links, artwork, saved state, duration, playing, shuffle, repeat, and which buttons are enabled. It carries no position, so it does not change every second. Null while it is closed.
- `ready.entity`, `entity` (value): kind, title, image, description, byline links, meta, monthly listeners, saved, following, and whether the page's play button shows Pause. `tracks` (list, virtualized, keyed by the row's `aria-labelledby`): title, artists or play count, image, URL, explicit and playing. `releases` (list): an artist's popular releases. The entity screen also reads `sections`.
- `ready.search`, `search` (value: the query from the URL, the filter chips with their checked state, whether recent searches can be cleared), `searchRows` (list keyed by the row's `aria-labelledby`: title, type, details, image, kind, explicit, verified and whether it can be dismissed; results and recent searches share it) and `browse` (list: the Browse all tiles with title, colour, image and hub URL). `ready.hub` waits for a hub's heading and first row.
- Commands: `setPlaying`, `skip({ direction })`, `setShuffle`, `setRepeat` (presses the button as often as the off, all, one cycle needs), `setTrackSaved`, `seek({ seconds })`, `openPlayer` and `closePlayer`. `playEntity`, `playTrack` and `openResult` close the full player first when it is open, so their tap lands on the page (decision-0019). The Connect prompt ("Listen on this phone") is found by its button's text, since Spotify shows it both in its client modal and in a layer of its own. Each is satisfied when the page already matches and otherwise waits for the page to show the change. Clicks that start or change playback are trusted input. `skip({ direction })` scrolls the mini-player carousel to its end for Next or its start for Previous, and is satisfied when the title changes and Spotify has rebuilt the row around the new track, so a burst sends each skip only after the one before has landed. Previous more than 3 s into a track, or a skip when the carousel has no neighbour, taps the full player's button instead (decision-0015). `seek({ seconds, title })` taps the full player's progress bar once, at the target's fraction of its width, and refuses when the title has changed since the user scrubbed (decision-0014; decision-0005's arrow keys are gone). Entity pages add `playEntity({ playing })`, `playTrack({ rowId, title })` (a trusted tap on the row's plain area, the point farthest from any link or button in it, so the tap plays the row and never opens a page), `setEntitySaved({ saved })`, `setFollowing({ following })`, `setRowLiked({ rowId, liked })` and `openRowPage({ rowId, page })` (each opens the website row's menu with a trusted tap on its More button and taps Add to Liked Songs, Remove from Liked Songs, View artist or View album; `setRowLiked` is satisfied when the row's heart matches, `openRowPage` when the address is an artist or album page) and `loadMoreTracks` (scrolls the last row into view and on to the end; a plain jump to the end loads nothing). Search adds `openSearch` (taps the page's search button), `setQuery({ query })` (fills Spotify's search input; Spotify searches as it changes and replaces the URL, so no page load happens per keystroke, where navigating by URL would reload the page), `setFilter({ label, checked })`, `openResult({ rowId })` (a trusted tap, which plays a song or opens a page and adds a recent search), `removeRecent({ rowId })` and `clearRecents`.

Playback routes built on 2026-11-19 (decisions 0014 to 0017):

- The playback store (`src/playback/store.ts`, plain TypeScript) merges `nowPlaying` and `player` with what the user asked for since, and `src/playback/use-playback.ts` connects it to React. The mini-player and Now Playing draw from it. It sends one command at a time. Identical skips queue and go out one after another; opposite intents (Play then Pause, Next then Previous) cancel before they are sent. While a skip is pending the view shows the next track's title and restarts the clock at 0. A command the page reports as not ready is retried after 300 ms. An intent Spotify has not confirmed after 6 s falls back to what Spotify shows, with one toast per burst. When the page briefly reports no track, as during a load, the store keeps the last track for up to 10 s. While the website loads a page, the store holds every request for up to 3 s and sends it once the page is ready, and requests do not age during the load. A request the page answers with `NOT_READY`, `CONTEXT_REPLACED` or `PRECONDITION_FAILED` is sent again, not reported (gap P-04). A skip is sent again only when nothing reached Spotify's button; a skip whose page went away after the click is dropped without a toast, because it may have landed. Sign-out clears the store. A row tap goes through the store too: the bar shows the row's title, artist and art at once, the row shows as playing, and a second row tap replaces the first, so the last one wins. The start is cleared when Spotify shows that title, or falls back after 6 s with a toast (gaps P-05, P-09). `loadMoreTracks` starts only once the page is ready and current, and a load cut off by a route change is asked again on the next scroll, not reported (gap O-03).
- The store holds `primary` at `running` while this page plays (decision-0011), through `useHold` in the playback feed the app shell mounts once.
- A track that ends by itself while a Next waits counts as that Next, so Next tapped as a track ends moves one track (2026-11-20, gap P-12). The store tells the page the title it skipped from, and the page treats a Next as done when that track has already ended.
- The position runs on a local clock from `nowPlaying.progress`; only the progress components update on each animation frame. This supersedes the busy-until-answer part of decision-0008 for playback controls (decision-0017); other actions keep it.
