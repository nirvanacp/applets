# Review fixes: dropped rows, long seeks, player open failure, empty filters

Problem ID: spotify-applet
Problem: Spotify applet over the web player, built from the PRD designs
Spirit animal: 🦜 Parrot

Fixes for the automated review of the milestone 1 to 3 pull request.

- `playTrack`: a row Spotify dropped after more rows loaded is scrolled back before the tap. The distance comes from the row's place (the `-<n>` ending of its id), the nearest drawn row and the row height. The screen passes the title, so the command can confirm playback once the row is gone again.
- `seek`: one call moves at most `SEEK_SECONDS_PER_CALL` (50 seconds), inside the host's 15-second call limit. Now Playing repeats the call until the position arrives, and stops on a failure.
- Now Playing: when `openPlayer` ends in anything but `observed`, the screen stops loading and shows the reason with Try again.
- Search: the filter chips also show when a filter finds no results, so the filter can be switched or cleared.

Verification: `tsc`, `build` and `genesis-applet check` pass. Not verified on the device. The phone was in use by its owner when these fixes were made.
