# Count a track's end as the waiting Next

Problem ID: `spotify-official-app-parity` · Problem: Make the Spotify applet match the official Spotify Android app in features and interaction speed · Spirit animal: 🐬 Dolphin

- Goal: Next tapped in the last moment of a track moves one track, as in the official app, and the rest of plan step 9 group 1 is settled (gaps P-12, N-04, N-05).
- Changes:
  - The playback store counts a track that ends by itself as the first Next still waiting, when no skip is on its way and none landed in the last 1.5 s. A burst of Nexts still moves once per tap.
  - The store tells the page's `skip` the title it skipped from, when the watch's title is current (no skip or row tap on its way). The page treats a Next as done when the track it names has already ended, so a Next that reaches the page after the track changed does not move a second time.
  - `current.md`: the native feel section no longer says nothing was checked on a device; the left-edge drag is listed as blocked by the platform.
- Design table: P-12 fixed; N-05 confirmed (fixed earlier by finding the prompt by its text); N-04 open, needs the shell to exclude applet elements from the system Back gesture.
- Verification:
  - `typecheck`, `check` and `build` pass with no warnings; revision 53 installed on the phone (Samsung SM-M307F).
  - Simulated against the bundled store: before the change, Next at a track's natural end moved from A to C; after it, to B. Five quick Nexts with a watch 60 ms late still end five tracks on.
  - On the phone, Next tapped with the track at 0.6 s and at 0 s left: both times the applet and the website ended one track on. In the first run the applet showed the track after for about 140 ms before settling, probably while the website's carousel slid; in the second it showed the right title 477 ms after the tap, and the website's own carousel showed a wrong slot for about 120 ms that the applet did not follow.
  - The Connect prompt: with a "Listen on this phone" layer added to the website for the probe and removed after, the mini-player read "Playing on another device" in green, never "Mobile Web Player".
  - The left-edge drag: in Now Playing, a drag from 6 px to 500 px along the progress bar closed Now Playing (system Back); the same drag from 120 px seeked and left it open.
