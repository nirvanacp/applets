# Answer playback controls at once

Problem ID: `spotify-official-app-parity` · Problem: Make the Spotify applet match the official Spotify Android app in features and interaction speed · Spirit animal: 🐬 Dolphin

- Goal: Make Play, Pause, Next, Previous, seek and save on the mini-player and Now Playing answer in the same frame as the tap and never drop a tap, as the official app does (gaps P-03, P-04, P-06 to P-09).
- Changes: A new playback store (`src/playback/store.ts`) and its hooks (`src/playback/use-playback.ts`) merge Spotify's readings with pending intents, queue and cancel taps, time intents out after 6 s with one toast, and keep the last track for 10 s through empty readings. The hold moved into the store's feed. `skip` scrolls the mini-player carousel and waits until Spotify rebuilds the row; `seek` is one tap on the progress bar. `nowPlaying` gained `next`, `previous` and `progress`; `player` lost its position. The mini-player gained a progress line. `MIN_BUSY_MS`, `useBusy` on playback controls and the player skeleton are gone.
- Decisions: decisions 0014 to 0017. Decision-0017 supersedes the busy part of decision-0008 for playback controls. Neighbour tracks carry titles only, because their artist lines lag.
- Verification: `check`, `typecheck` and `build` pass with no warnings. On the phone: Play/Pause feedback 90 to 181 ms, the next title in 74 to 100 ms, five Next taps 250 ms apart advanced five tracks, and a seek landed on its target second, confirmed 620 ms after the tap. The first burst run advanced three tracks for five taps, because a skip counted as done as soon as the scroll moved the row; it now waits for Spotify's rebuild. Not checked: the Connect prompt, a track ending during a pending skip, the last track of a queue, ads on Free.
