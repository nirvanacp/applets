# Trusted tap for cards without a link

Problem ID: spotify-applet
Problem: Spotify applet over the web player, built from the PRD designs
Spirit animal: 🦜 Parrot

- Shelf cards with no link, such as the playlists on a genre hub, did nothing when tapped: Spotify ignored the synthetic click on the card title. `src/components/shelf.tsx` now sends a trusted tap (`input: "trusted"`) to the card itself.
