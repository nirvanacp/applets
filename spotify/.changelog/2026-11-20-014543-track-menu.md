# Add the track menu

Problem ID: `spotify-official-app-parity` · Problem: Make the Spotify applet match the official Spotify Android app in features and interaction speed · Spirit animal: 🐬 Dolphin

- Goal: a track row's menu, drawn as a sheet like the official app's, with the items the Free web player's row menu has (plan step 9 group 3, gap F-07).
- Changes:
  - Each album, playlist, artist and Liked Songs row has a ⋮ button, and a long press opens the same sheet. The sheet has Add to Liked Songs or Remove from Liked Songs, Share, View artist and View album. A page hides its link to itself.
  - Like closes the sheet and the row shows the new state at once, with a green tick on liked rows. The toast confirms, with Undo after a removal. View artist and View album show a spinner until the page opens.
  - New page commands `setRowLiked` and `openRowPage` open the website row's menu with a trusted tap and tap the item. Rows now read their liked state from the website's heart.
- Decisions: View track is left out because it opens a track page the applet does not draw. Undo stays on Liked Songs, because the website keeps an unliked row until the page reloads.
- Verification: `typecheck`, `check` and `build` pass with no warnings; on the phone (revisions 56 and 57) the ⋮ button, long press, like, unlike from an album and from Liked Songs, View artist, View album, Share (dismissed with Back) and closing by Back and by drag all worked.
