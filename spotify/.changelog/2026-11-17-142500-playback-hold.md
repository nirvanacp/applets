# Hold the Spotify page while it plays

Problem ID: `applet-performance` · Problem: Find what makes page switches, loads and like/save actions lag in the built applets · Spirit animal: 🐆 Leopard

- Goal: Keep music and the now-playing display correct now that Genesis rests a covered page the applet is not using, and cut the cost of Home's updates.
- Changes: `usePlaybackHold` in `src/components/playback.ts` holds the `primary` context at `running` while `nowPlaying` says this page plays; the app shell calls it once. `readSection` returns at most 20 cards per shelf.
- Decisions: decision-0011. The hold follows the page's own playing state instead of the Play tap, so it also covers playback the website starts and ends when another device takes over.
- Verification: `check`, `typecheck` and `build` pass. Before the change, on a phone with the page resting: audio continued, a track change was followed by 13 s of silence, and the mini-player stayed on the old track. After it, on the same phone (Samsung SM-M307F, Android 11): the hold was requested when audio started and released on pause; at a track change the gap was 0.3 s, the same as with resting turned off, and the mini-player showed the new track 0.4 s later. Playing under the hold cost about 1.4 s of CPU per second against 2.4 with resting off; after pause it fell to 10 to 20 ms. Home's shelves show at most 20 cards. Seen and not changed: the hold drops for about 0.2 s when a navigation resets the playing state, and audio began about 8 s after the first Play tap.
