# Bars move away while scrolling; Home's header is X's

Problem ID: `x-official-app-parity` · Problem: Make the X applet look and feel like the official X Android app · Spirit animal: 🦜 Parrot

- Goal: Let posts fill the screen while scrolling, as X's app does, and give Home X's own header (G-004, G-005). Keep posts' pictures after lists load far ahead.
- Changes: on tab roots the top bar and the floating tab bar slide away on a scroll down and return on a scroll up, near the top, or on a change of screen. Home's top bar shows the account's avatar, which opens Profile, X's mark in the middle and Refresh. A new `selfAvatar` reader reads the avatar from X's Home page. The feed keeps the avatar and media it last saw for each post when X's recycled cells read without them.
- Decisions: decision-0023, decision-0024.
- Verification: `typecheck`, `genesis-applet check` and `build` pass with no warnings. On the phone, both bars leave and return, the avatar opens Profile, and after ten flings the top posts keep their avatars and videos. Not checked: the bars on Explore and Profile roots.
