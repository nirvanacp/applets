# Feed videos at their shape, played in X's viewer

Problem ID: `x-official-app-parity` · Problem: Make the X applet look and feel like the official X Android app · Spirit animal: 🦜 Parrot

- Goal: Replace the "Video · Play on X" tile with a video that looks like X's app (G-001, G-003 of the parity plan).
- Changes: the post reader returns each video's shape, length and, for GIFs, the MP4 file, from the DOM only. Feed rows draw the poster at that shape with X's play button and the length; a tap opens X's own full-screen viewer at `/status/<id>/video/1`. GIFs play inline, silent and looping, one at a time. The applet page sends no referrer, which X's video host needs. The old tile stays for a video the reader can't describe.
- Decisions: decision-0021. Videos other than GIFs don't autoplay in the feed; the decision says why.
- Verification: `typecheck`, `genesis-applet check` and `build` pass with no warnings. On the phone: Home shows posters at their shape with lengths, a tap opens X's viewer, it plays with sound, and Back returns to the same feed position. Not checked: a GIF in the feed (none appeared).
