# Review X's contexts and timeline reader for cost

Problem ID: `applet-performance` · Problem: Find what makes page switches, loads and like/save actions lag in the built applets · Spirit animal: 🐆 Leopard

- Goal: Decide whether the `account` context and the unbounded timeline list should change, now that Genesis rests pages the applet is not using.
- Changes: none in code. The decision is recorded.
- Decisions: decision-0019 keeps the three contexts and the timeline reader.
- Verification: on a phone with the installed build: Home, Explore and a post with its replies open on the first tap, and Back works. Idle on Home costs 4 to 30 ms per second, the cold open 8.5 to 11.2 s. One open point: after Back from a post the page worked for about 40 s (620 to 650 ms per second) with no touches; the cause is not known. Likes and bookmarks were not tapped.
