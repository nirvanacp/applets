## decision-0003: Three contexts

- Date: 2026-11-13.
- Choice: `home` (start `/`) for Home, Profile and Notifications; `explore` (start `/explore/`) for Search and Reels; `direct` (start `/direct/inbox/`) for Messages. Genesis allows at most three contexts, and these keep the busiest tabs' histories apart.
- Evidence: revised in phase 1. `/reels/` redirects to one reel and plays as the website (decision-0006), so it no longer anchors a context; Explore is the Search tab's home page.

## decision-0006: Video plays on the website

- Date: 2026-11-13.
- Choice: the Reels tab and the story viewer show instagram.com itself (`website: true` routes for `/reels/…` and `/stories/…`). Feed, post and grid videos show their poster image with a play badge; tapping one opens the post or reel on the website. The applet's stories tray stays custom and opens a story on the website.
- Evidence: phase 1 inspection. Every `video` on instagram.com plays a `blob:` Media Source stream that only exists inside the website's document, so the applet UI cannot play it. `/stories/<user>/` also shows a "View as <user>?" interstitial that the website owns.

## decision-0007: Final context ids and Reels in the primary context

- Date: 2026-11-13.
- Origin: Claude, during implementation.
- Choice: the contexts are `primary` (start `/`), `explore` (start `/explore/`) and `direct` (start `/direct/inbox/`). Home, Notifications, Profile and the Reels tab use `primary`; Search uses `explore`; Messages uses `direct`. Supersedes the context names and the Reels placement of decision-0003.
- Rationale: the scaffold's `primary` id was kept. Reels shows the website (decision-0006), so it needs no context of its own; opening it in `primary` keeps Search's history apart.
- Affects: tab selection in `src/app.tsx`, route contexts in `src/routes.ts`.

## decision-0008: No applet storage

- Date: 2026-11-13.
- Origin: Claude, during implementation.
- Choice: the applet keeps no storage. The feed variant lives in the URL (`?variant=`) and Instagram's search keeps its own recent searches. Supersedes decision-0004.
- Rationale: both preferences already have an owner on instagram.com, so a copy could only disagree with it.

## decision-0009: Done from a viewer, and reload on resume only

- Date: 2026-11-13.
- Origin: Claude, from device verification.
- Choice: Done steps the website back as usual. When that step lands in the story or Reels viewer again, the applet goes to Home after 1.5 s. The feed reloads when the app returns from the background, not on Done.
- Rationale: `useAppResume` also fires on Done, and its `context.reload` cancelled Done's `context.back`, so Done did nothing on every website page. The viewers can hold several entries of their own.
- Affects: journeys "Story ring", "Reels tab", "Unrouted page, Done".

## decision-0011: Keep the three contexts until a signed-in check

- Date and origin: 2026-11-17; agent choice. Problem ID: `applet-performance` · Problem: Find what makes page switches, loads and like/save actions lag in the built applets · Spirit animal: 🐆 Leopard
- Finding: Genesis now starts an applet's pages one after another and rests each page the applet is not using. The phone used for the check was signed out of Instagram, so only the sign-in page was seen.
- Choice and rationale: `explore` and `direct` stay. They are the Search and Messages tabs with their own histories. No hold is added: whether reels or stories play in a covered page was not checked.
- Rules and journeys: Search, Messages, reels and stories.
- Evidence and supersession: none; the review is open until a signed-in check. None superseded.

## decision-0012: Show kept pages first, keep Home, never keep messages

- Date and origin: 2026-11-18; agent choice while upgrading the applet to Genesis's runtime speed work. Problem ID: `applet-performance` · Problem: Find what makes page switches, loads and like/save actions lag in the built applets · Spirit animal: 🐆 Leopard
- Choice and rationale: screens keep drawing content whenever there is data, fresh or kept, and read empty and failed from the watch's data state (`watchStatus`). Home's `feed` and `stories` and the shell's `me` set `persist`, because Home is the first screen and the Profile tab shows the account's picture. The inbox and chats never set it: they hold direct messages. When the router reports no account and the applet last saw one, the applet calls `forgetCached`; it remembers only "signed in" or "signed out" in its own storage, so a signed-out start does not forget each time. No list uses `VirtualList`: the feed grows by a few tall posts per Show more and Instagram limits how often it answers, so it rarely nears 50 posts; its pictures have no known size until they load, so measured heights would move while Back restores the position; profile and Explore grids are three tiles to a row, and the virtual list draws one row per key.
- Rules and journeys: Home, Profile tab, Messages, sign out.
- Evidence and supersession: [screen helpers](../src/screen.ts), [Home](../src/screens/home.tsx), [app](../src/app.tsx). None superseded.
