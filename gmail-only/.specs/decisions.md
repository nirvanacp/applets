## decision-0002: Build the To-do & Reminders brief over Gmail

- Date and origin: 2026-11-09, user instruction with the feature brief "To-do & Reminders from Email" and 14 mockups.
- Choice and rationale: Gmail Only is a full Gmail client with reminders, to-dos and snoozes saved from emails. The mockups set the palette (accent `#2347d9`, near-black `#15161a`, reminder orange `#9a3d06` on `#fdebdc`, red `#c62828`, wash `#f4f5f7`) and the screens: inbox with the Today card, "Add to…" menu, reminder and to-do forms, item detail, the To-do tab in two views, bulk "Save for later", compose with "Remind me if no reply", search and settings. The brief's "Not in version 1" list stays out.
- Evidence and supersession: [current spec](current.md), [manifest](../applet.json). Supersedes the `home` route of decision-0001.

## decision-0003: One route and screen for all of Gmail's mobile web

- Date and origin: 2026-11-09, agent choice to meet the user's rule that no Gmail page is left without a custom screen.
- Choice and rationale: Gmail's mobile web is one page whose hash picks a view (`#tl`, `#cv`, `#co`, `#mn`, `#pr`). One `mail` route covers `/mail/*` and `/`; the adapter reports the visible view and the `mail` screen draws the matching applet view. A view the applet does not know gets a themed screen that offers Gmail itself, so no Gmail page shows unstyled. Navigation uses Gmail's own URLs, so Back follows Gmail's history in the context.
- Evidence and supersession: [adapter pages](../src/adapter/pages.ts), [mail screen](../src/screens/mail.tsx). Supersedes none.

## decision-0004: Reminders live in applet storage; snooze is the applet's

- Date and origin: 2026-11-09, agent choice under platform and website limits.
- Choice and rationale: Items are stored in Genesis storage (`items`), not as Gmail labels, so saving works offline and never changes the account. Each item keeps a copy of the email's sender, subject and snippet, so a deleted email still shows ("Email deleted"). Gmail's mobile web has no snooze, so Snooze archives the email and stores a time; when it is due, the email is listed first in the inbox under "Back in your inbox", and the Snoozed list is in the drawer. "Bring email back" works the same way for reminders.
- Evidence and supersession: [store](../src/store.ts), [model](../src/model.ts), [add sheet](../src/screens/add.tsx). Supersedes none.

## decision-0005: Due items show inside the app, not as phone notifications

- Date and origin: 2026-11-09, agent choice under a platform limit; not approved by the user as a deferral.
- Choice and rationale: Genesis applets cannot schedule Android notifications, so the brief's section 9 is met inside the app: the To-do badge counts due today plus overdue, the Today card replaces the morning summary, reminder chips mark inbox rows and "Back in your inbox" replaces the fired reminder. The settings screen says so. The "notify me" and morning summary settings are stored so a later platform feature can use them.
- Evidence and supersession: [app shell](../src/app.tsx), [settings](../src/screens/settings.tsx). Supersedes none. Open for the user.

## decision-0006: Multi-account built and switched off

- Date and origin: 2026-11-09, user instruction: write multi-account support now, enable and test it later with a second account.
- Choice and rationale: Every item stores its `accountId`; accounts have a name, color, Gmail session index (`/mail/u/<index>/`) and "Show in inbox" and "Show in To-do" switches; the To-do filter, account dots, account setup and the switcher are all built. `MULTI_ACCOUNT_ENABLED` in `src/model.ts` only hides the entry points that add or switch to a second Google session, and account dots on inbox rows appear only when more than one account exists. Turning the flag on is the only code change needed to test it.
- Evidence and supersession: [model](../src/model.ts), [accounts](../src/screens/accounts.tsx), [account setup](../src/screens/account-setup.tsx). Supersedes none.

## decision-0007: Light and dark themes from one token set

- Date and origin: 2026-11-09, agent choice following the user's request for a consistent theme and the project's design guidance.
- Choice and rationale: Colors are CSS tokens on `:root` with dark values under `prefers-color-scheme` and `data-theme`. The Theme setting follows the system or forces one. Targets are at least 48dp. The Today card has its own `--hero` tokens so it stays a solid blue card in light mode and a deep navy card in dark mode.

## decision-0008: Compose body is written in Gmail

- Date and origin: 2026-11-09, agent choice under a website limit.
- Choice and rationale: The applet fills Gmail's To, Cc, Bcc and Subject fields as the person types. Gmail's message body is a rich-text field Genesis cannot fill reliably, so "Continue in Gmail" shows Gmail's own compose to write and send; the applet never sends on its own. "Remind me if no reply" saves its follow-up to-do at that point. Close uses Gmail's own Close so Gmail returns to the list it came from.
- Evidence and supersession: [compose](../src/screens/compose.tsx). Supersedes none.

## decision-0009: Motion and feedback for every change on screen

- Date and origin: 2026-11-09, user instruction to add transitions, animations and loaders so the applet stops feeling rigid.
- Choice and rationale: Screens move by where they sit: a deeper history entry or overlay pushes in from the end edge, Back pops, Mail and To-do fade through, and the drawer slides from the start edge. Genesis swaps screens at once, so each screen slot is a `Stage` that copies the outgoing screen's DOM and plays its exit as an inert ghost while the live screen enters; taps and Back never wait. Sheets rise, follow a drag and sink from where they are; popovers grow from their button. Loading has three steps: nothing for 120 ms, then a shimmer skeleton shaped like the content, then a fade-in; work on content already shown runs a thin progress bar. Star and selection show the requested state through `usePending`. Completing an item pops its check, waits a moment, then leaves with an Undo toast. Durations stay at 150 to 350 ms, and with reduced motion everything swaps at once.
- Evidence and supersession: [motion](../src/components/motion.tsx), [app shell](../src/app.tsx), [theme](../src/theme.css), [list](../src/screens/list.tsx). Supersedes none.

## decision-0010: Multiple accounts turned on, sessions addressed by address

- Date and origin: 2026-11-09, user instruction to set up the multi-account feature and test it with two accounts.
- Choice and rationale: `MULTI_ACCOUNT_ENABLED` is true. Gmail's mobile URLs carry no `/u/<index>/`, so the adapter reads the session index from the page, and the store corrects an account's index when it differs. Switching and opening a saved email use `/mail/mu/?authuser=<address>`, which Gmail resolves to that session even after Google renumbers sessions on a sign-out; the index is the fallback. Opening an item from another account switches to its session. "Show in inbox" decides whether an account's due items appear on the Today card. Unread counts clear on a switch. Search covers the open account only, so its placeholder says "Search in mail".
- Evidence and supersession: [model](../src/model.ts), [store](../src/store.ts), [mail navigation](../src/components/mail.ts), [accounts](../src/screens/accounts.tsx). Supersedes the "switched off" part of decision-0006.

## decision-0011: Undoing a swipe archive goes through All mail

- Date and origin: 2026-11-09, agent choice under a website limit, from the phase-12 everyday features plan.
- Choice and rationale: Gmail's own archive Undo link stays live for about 3 s and does nothing once hidden, which is shorter than the applet's toast. Gmail's mobile web offers "Move to inbox" only on rows of a list that keeps archived mail, so the Undo opens All mail, waits up to 10 s for the archived row on the first page, runs `threads.moveToInbox`, and goes back with "Moved back to inbox". The row's "Inbox" chip is the evidence that the move happened. If the row does not show up, or Gmail refuses, a toast tells the person to open the email and use Move to inbox.
- Evidence and supersession: [adapter pages](../src/adapter/pages.ts), [mail navigation](../src/components/mail.ts), [list](../src/screens/list.tsx). Supersedes none.

## decision-0012: Gmail's close question is asked in the applet and answered through history

- Date and origin: 2026-11-09, agent choice under a website limit, from the phase-12 everyday features plan.
- Choice and rationale: Closing a draft with content makes Gmail ask Discard, Save or Cancel in a sheet outside its compose view, which the applet hid. The compose page now rereads when that sheet appears and shows the same three choices in the applet's own sheet, then runs Gmail's button. Gmail's Discard and Save open the email as a new history entry after compose, so Back from the email reopened compose; after either choice the applet goes back twice, past the compose entry. Gmail's own Close on an empty draft opens whichever view Gmail last showed, sometimes an email instead of the list, so an empty draft closes with Back, which drops it.
- Evidence and supersession: [adapter pages](../src/adapter/pages.ts), [adapter read](../src/adapter/read.ts), [compose](../src/screens/compose.tsx). Supersedes none.

## decision-0013: Email commands run through Gmail's own panel, and Back skips the email Gmail leaves behind

- Date and origin: 2026-11-09, agent choice under website limits, from Step 5 of the phase-12 everyday features plan.
- Choice and rationale: Gmail's mobile toolbar shows only some commands; the rest (Mute, Move to inbox, Report spam, Mark as unread) live in a More options panel that Gmail builds on first open with its own copies, so a command the toolbar hides opens the panel and clicks the panel's copy. The toolbar shows only the commands Gmail offers for that email. After a command Gmail leaves the email in its history, so Back goes past it to the list. Gmail opens an email under the list it showed last and adds a history entry for a URL under another list, so a saved email opens under that last list. A snoozed email that is opened after its time has come ends its snooze.
- Evidence and supersession: [adapter pages](../src/adapter/pages.ts), [mail navigation](../src/components/mail.ts), [model](../src/model.ts), [snoozed](../src/screens/snoozed.tsx); commits `aa97a7f`, `2f93fe6`, `ebf4ddf`. Supersedes none.
