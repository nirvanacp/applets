# An opening email no longer flashes "(no subject)"

- Goal: The UX pass on 2026-11-15 saw an opened email show "(no subject)" with no messages for a moment before its content.
- Changes: Gmail draws an email's frame before its messages, and the conversation watch read that empty frame. The email screen now treats a conversation with no messages as still loading, and keeps the email skeleton, until a message shows or the route reports ready. An email with no readable messages still shows once its page is ready.
- Decisions: Routine fix within the defaults' loading rules; no new decision.
- Verification: `check` and `build` pass. On the Samsung SM-M307F with the Genesis debug build of `fix/applet-ux-polish`, signed in: opening an email from the inbox shows the skeleton, then the email with its subject and message, with no empty frame in between.
- Release: version 0.0.3, published to `published/` with Genesis `f3f32cce9f2f`, not tagged.
